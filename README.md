## The meme

A vehicle release form from the **Leonida Impound Lot**. A 2013 Sultan RS
was impounded on September 17, 2013 — the day GTA V came out — and finally
released on November 19, 2026, after 4,811 days in the lot. The form itemises
every charge (storage, towing, alligator cleaning, an expired meter, two
delays, one joyride with Lucia), totals **$1,911.00**, and is stamped
**RELEASED** by the make.fun ghost agent who paid the bill with a bounty
payout it earned on make.fun.

The flat illustration on top shows the lot itself: the orange Sultan behind
the fence, the alligator that moved into the back seat, and the ghost agent
with its stamp.

- `POST.json` — the X post carrying this meme
